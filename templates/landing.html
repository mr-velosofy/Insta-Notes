FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q. Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no logins, and no data leaves your browser. Just open the studio and start creating.

Q. Where is my audio uploaded?

Nowhere. Recording, editing, and rendering all happen on your device. Close the tab and everything is gone.

Q. What formats are supported?

Upload MP3, WAV, M4A or OGG audio, or record straight from your microphone. Export your video as MP4.

Q. Is there a length limit?

Voice notes are capped at 3 minutes so they stay punchy and load fast. You can trim any upload to the perfect length.

Q. Can I use my own photo as the avatar?

Yes — drop any image onto the preview or use the built-in picker. The avatar is rendered right into your exported video.

Q. Does it work offline?

The app itself runs entirely in your browser. Only the Inter font is loaded from Google's CDN; everything else is local.
